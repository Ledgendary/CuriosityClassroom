Our Story

Igniting young minds, one adventure at a time

At The Curiosity Classroom, we believe learning should be an exciting adventure. Based in Churchdown, our tutoring is the heartfelt creation of Kirsty Haines, an experienced educator who understands that every child learns uniquely.

We don't just teach the curriculum. We nurture curiosity, foster problem-solving and grow a genuine love of learning in a supportive environment. Your child isn't just another student here. They're a young explorer on a journey built specifically for them.

Parents consistently tell us their once-reluctant learners now eagerly look forward to their sessions, growing in confidence both academically and personally.

Kirsty Haines Founder, The Curiosity Classroom

Learning Principles

How we make learning stick

Three simple promises behind every single session.

🧭

Their own path

Every child is unique. We tailor each session to your child's learning style, strengths and interests, with exactly the right support to help them flourish.

🚀

Excel through play

Prize wheels, Lego fractions and sweet-shop maths deliver real curriculum mastery through games that captivate. Children exceed expectations because they're having too much fun to give up.

✨

Confidence beyond books

A warm, supportive space where questions are celebrated and progress is cheered. The resilience and self-belief children build here follow them into school and beyond.
