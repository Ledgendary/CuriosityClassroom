Our Story

Play-Based Tutoring That Builds Confidence and Makes Learning Feel Possible

At The Curiosity Classroom, I believe that learning should be an exciting adventure! Based in Churchdown, I created The Curiosity Classroom to provide personalised, hands-on tutoring for primary school children in a warm and supportive environment. Using a play-based approach, I design engaging learning experiences that spark curiosity, build confidence, and support progress in reading, writing and maths. Every child learns differently, so sessions are carefully tailored to each individual, helping them feel successful, capable and motivated to learn.

What makes The Curiosity Classroom special is the genuine connection I build with every child. I don't just teach the curriculum - I nurture curiosity, encourage problem-solving, and help children develop a positive growth mindset. Your child isn't just another student here; they're a young explorer on their own unique learning journey.

My aim is to transform traditional tutoring into something children actually look forward to. Parents often tell me how their once-reluctant learners now arrive excited for their sessions and leave feeling proud of what they have achieved, with growing confidence both academically and personally. If you're looking for more than just academic support - if you want your child to experience the joy and excitement of learning - I would love to welcome you to The Curiosity Classroom.

Kirsty Haines Founder, The Curiosity Classroom

Our Approach

How We Make Learning Meaningful

Three principles that shape the way I support every child.

No. 01

Learning Shaped Around Your Child

Every child is unique. Sessions are carefully tailored to their individual needs, strengths, interests and learning style, with the right balance of support and challenge.

No. 02

Learning Through Play

Games, practical resources and hands-on activities help make learning engaging and meaningful. Children can explore new ideas, practise important skills and develop their understanding without it feeling like more school.

No. 03

Confidence at the Heart

Children learn best when they feel safe, supported and able to have a go. I celebrate effort and progress, helping children develop confidence, resilience and a more positive relationship with learning.

Tutoring Options

Pick your adventure

After-School Tuition

One-to-One Tuition

Fun, hands-on sessions personally tailored to your child.

  • Sessions can focus on specific gaps, confidence, extra challenge or individual learning targets.
  • Where appropriate, tuition can support targets identified through MyPlans and EHCPs.

Small-Group Tuition

Fun, collaborative sessions based on year-group objectives.

  • Groups are kept small so each child receives individual support and appropriate challenge.
  • Children can build confidence, practise key skills and learn alongside others in an encouraging environment.

Daytime Tuition

EOTAS and Alternative Provision

Personalised daytime tuition for children who may find a traditional school environment difficult to access.

  • Play-based, hands-on learning to help lower barriers, rebuild confidence and encourage engagement.
  • Provision can be tailored around EHCP or MyPlan targets and may be funded through a school, the local authority or an EOTAS package.

Home Education

Every family's home education journey is different, and I can become part of that journey in a way that works for you.

  • Sessions are tailored around your child's individual needs, interests and learning style.
  • Families can choose the subjects, skills or areas they would like to focus on, while keeping learning practical and enjoyable.

Holiday Sessions

Fun, one-off learning sessions during the school holidays.

  • A relaxed and social way to keep learning ticking over and try something new.
  • No weekly commitment, making them a great way to experience The Curiosity Classroom.
